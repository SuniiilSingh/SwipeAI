🛡️ Zero-Tolerance UGC & Safety Rules

Last Updated: October 2026 • Shield 360 & App Store Guideline 1.2 Compliant

🚨 Zero-Tolerance Policy Notice

Blunderr Dating enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against objectionable user-generated content (UGC), harassment, exploitation, and abuse. Any user engaging in prohibited conduct will face immediate permanent account suspension and hardware device ban.

1. Prohibited Content & Behavior

You agree to adhere to these community standards at all times. The following are strictly forbidden on Blunderr Dating:

  • Explicit Nudity & Sexual Content: Uploading or sending explicit nudity, pornographic images, or sexually suggestive media.
  • Hate Speech & Discrimination: Content promoting violence, hatred, or discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.
  • Harassment & Bullying: Threatening, stalking, intimidating, insulting, or repeatedly sending unwelcome messages to any user.
  • Commercial Solicitation & Prostitution: Offering or soliciting escort services, prostitution, commercial sexual activity, or financial scams.
  • Fake Identity & Impersonation: Using stolen photos, creating false profiles, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Extortion & Unsolicited Media: Sharing private content without consent, blackmailing, or spamming unsolicited links.

2. Moderation & 24-Hour Review Action

Blunderr Dating utilizes real-time automated AI content filters (including automated image nudity detection) alongside dedicated human Trust & Safety moderators. All reported content or user profiles are investigated and acted upon within 24 hours of submission.

3. In-App User Controls (Block & Report)

Every profile card and active chat lounge features built-in safety controls:

  • Report Profile / Chat: Tap the 🚨 icon on any profile or chat screen to submit an instant report to our safety team.
  • Block User: Instantly block any user to prevent them from ever viewing your profile or contacting you again.
  • Unmatch: Sever connection and permanently remove the chat lounge at any time.

4. Real-World Date Safety Guidelines

When meeting matches in person, always prioritize your safety:

  • Meet in Public Venues: Always arrange initial dates in well-populated public places (or use our verified Safe Date Spots).
  • Share Date Details: Inform a trusted friend or family member of your location and plans. Use the app's Safe Date SOS button to share emergency tracking links.
  • Control Your Transportation: Arrange your own transportation to and from the venue.
  • Never Send Money: Never transfer money, share bank details, or fulfill financial requests from online matches.
